Joining Your Organization

To join an existing organization on Bank of Skill, please contact your organization's administrator. Access to organization accounts is managed exclusively by the organization's designated administrators.

Administrators can provide access in several ways, including:

  • Sending you an invitation to join the organization.
  • Creating your account manually.
  • Enabling Single Sign-On (SSO), allowing you to access the platform using your organization's login credentials and dedicated access link.

If you need access to your organization's Bank of Skill workspace, please reach out to one of your organization's administrators and request an account or invitation.

Once your access has been granted, you will be able to complete your profile, showcase your skills, track achievements, and explore new opportunities within the Bank of Skill ecosystem.
